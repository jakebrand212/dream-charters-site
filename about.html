We're a family that loves this lake

Dream Charters ATX is a family-owned charter operation built on one idea: give people the best possible day on the water and take care of every detail so they don't have to.

Captain's on us. Fuel's on us. You just bring the crew.

We keep it simple. Every charter comes with a licensed captain who handles the driving, the safety and the surf coaching — plus fuel, life jackets, boards, a tube, a cooler with ice and waters, and a premium sound system. No experience needed and nothing to haul.

What we're proudest of is that guests remember our captains by name. Scroll our reviews and you'll see the same names again and again — Tyler, Luke and Phoenix — because taking great care of people is the whole job.
